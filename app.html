⚠️ Hazard & Safety Guide

Mandatory Safety Protocols under E-Waste Rules 2022

🚫 No Open Cable Burning

Burning wires releases toxic dioxins and ruins copper purity. Handover intact cables to authorized yards.

🚫 No Acid Leaching of PCBs

Extracting gold with acid causes toxic fumes and cyanide poisoning. Formal smelters extract 98% safely.

⚠️ Safe Battery Handling

Never puncture or crush lithium batteries. Store in a cool, dry bin away from direct sun to prevent fires.
